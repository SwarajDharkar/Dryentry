03 / Working principle

Forced convection: A blower directs moving air over the wet canopy. The airflow renews the boundary layer and supports evaporation.

Droplet detachment: Air exerts aerodynamic drag on surface droplets. If the force is sufficient to overcome adhesion and surface-tension effects, droplets can detach and drain away.

Water collection: A sloped chamber floor guides detached water through a drain into a removable sump or tank.

Fd ≈ ½ ρ Cd A v²

Simplified aerodynamic drag relation. Actual droplet removal depends on droplet size, fabric, canopy shape and local airflow.

04 / Process sequence

  1. Insert wet umbrella into the chamber.
  2. Close the access door and start the cycle.
  3. Blower sends air through directed outlets.
  4. Detached water falls to the sloped base.
  5. Drainage channel directs water into the collection tank.
  6. Remove umbrella after the cycle completes.
